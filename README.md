# AM 轉職羅盤 (AM Compass)

A mobile-friendly planner for a Mighty DAO Talent Bridge Account Manager who is moving into a web3 career.

**Live:** https://nathanc90.github.io/am-compass/

- **今日**: today's tasks, overdue catch-ups, and the deadline cheat sheet (12h / 24h / 72h / 48h)
- **行事曆**: month calendar of AM updates (Thu/Sun), AL syncs (Mon/Fri), plan deadlines and study blocks
- **轉職計畫**: 26-week career plan checklist, with due dates calculated from your start date
- **測驗**: 12-question self-quiz on the account workflow
- **知識庫**: the 8-step workflow, AM vs AL roles, and a searchable Chinese–English web3 glossary

A single `index.html` with no build step. Progress is saved in your browser's `localStorage`, so it stays on the device you use and is lost if you clear site data.
